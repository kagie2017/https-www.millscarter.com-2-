Product sourcing, development and production

The right supplier shapes your margins, your quality and your speed to market. We find that supplier and manage production for you.

Sourcing sits at the heart of your supply chain

Where you buy affects your bottom line and how well your product does in the market. Good sourcing depends on the product, the market and, most of all, on reliable partners who deliver quality at a competitive price.

Whether you produce close to home or across borders, we help you find those partners and build a supply base that improves profitability and lets you sell in more markets.

What’s included

  • Finding the right factory

    We search, vet and compare manufacturers, then negotiate terms, so you only see options that meet your standards.

  • Development and samples

    From concept and design to prototypes and samples. We manage revisions until the product is right.

  • Production and quality

    We follow full-scale production on site and inspect against your specification before anything ships.
