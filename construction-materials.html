Construction materials supply

Building materials sourced from vetted manufacturers, checked for quality and delivered to your site or warehouse. One partner from quote to delivery.

Materials for every stage of a build

Construction projects run on schedules, and late or wrong materials hold everything up. We source the materials on your list, compare suppliers on price and quality, and coordinate delivery so they arrive when the site needs them.

Examples of what we supply:

  • Structural steel, rebar and mesh
  • Cement, concrete blocks and paving
  • Timber, panels and boards
  • Tiles, flooring and wall finishes
  • Doors, windows and hardware
  • Sanitary ware, fixtures and fittings
  • Insulation and roofing materials

Need something that isn’t listed? Send us the specification and we’ll look for it.

How we support your project

  • Bulk and project orders

    Full container loads for large projects, or mixed materials consolidated into one shipment to save on freight.

  • Quality and compliance

    We check materials against your specification and the standards your market requires before anything ships.

  • Delivered on schedule

    We plan production, freight and documents around your build programme, with storage in our warehouse when you need it.

How an order runs

The same four steps we use for every product.

  1. 1

    Brief

    You tell us what you need: the product, quantities, target price, timelines and where it has to go.

  2. 2

    Source and sample

    We match you with vetted manufacturers, compare offers and manage prototypes and samples until you approve.

  3. 3

    Produce and inspect

    We follow production on the ground and check quality against your specification before goods leave the factory.

  4. 4

    Store and deliver

    Goods move into our warehouse or straight to you, by air or sea, with the paperwork handled.
