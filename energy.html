Energy and power equipment

From standby generators to complete power packages. We source energy equipment from trusted manufacturers and deliver it ready for your market.

Generators sourced to your specification

Whether you need backup power for a building, prime power for a remote site or units to resell, we match you with manufacturers that build to your requirements, then handle inspection, shipping and paperwork.

Examples of what we supply:

  • Diesel generators
  • Gas generators
  • Portable and mobile units
  • Silent (canopy) and containerised generators
  • Automatic transfer switches and control panels
  • Spare parts and service kits

Choosing the right generator

The right unit depends on how often it will run and where.

TypeWhat it’s forTypical use
StandbyTakes over automatically when the grid failsOffices, hospitals, data rooms, shops
PrimeMain power source where there is no reliable gridConstruction sites, mines, remote operations
PortableShort-term power that moves with the jobEvents, maintenance work, tools on site

What we take care of

  • Built for your market

    Voltage, frequency and certification matched to where the equipment will run, such as 230/400 V at 50 Hz in Europe.

  • Checked before shipping

    Factory test reports and documentation reviewed, and units inspected against your order before they leave.

  • Heavy freight handled

    Generators are large and heavy. We book the right container, handle the paperwork and can store units in our warehouse.

Need power equipment?

Tell us the output you need (kVA), the fuel type and where it will be used. We’ll find the right unit.
