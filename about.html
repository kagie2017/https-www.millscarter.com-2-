Global efficiency, local expertise

Mills Carter is a one-stop outsourcing partner. We source, develop and produce products for our clients, then store and ship them anywhere in the world.

What we do

We navigate the global manufacturing landscape so you don’t have to.

Outsourcing production should lower your costs, not your standards. We give businesses access to a network of vetted manufacturing partners, and we stay involved from the first brief until the goods arrive.

Emerging markets give you cost-effective production. Established centres give you innovation and speed. We know both, and we help you choose the right mix for each product.

How we’re set up

A global infrastructure that works like a local team.

  • Five offices, one team

    Our five offices act as hubs close to where products are made, so someone is always on the ground.

  • Based in Denmark

    Headquartered in Vallensbæk, just outside Copenhagen, with European standards built into how we work.

  • One contact for you

    A dedicated account manager is your single point of contact for suppliers, production, storage and freight.

What you can expect

  • Transparency

    Clear prices, honest timelines and straight answers about your suppliers and your orders.

  • Quality assurance

    A strict quality management system and inspections that keep every order aligned with your specification.

  • Support 24/7

    Questions don’t wait for office hours. Our team is reachable around the clock.

How a project runs

Four steps from idea to delivery. Join at any step.

  1. 1

    Brief

    You tell us what you need: the product, quantities, target price, timelines and where it has to go.

  2. 2

    Source and sample

    We match you with vetted manufacturers, compare offers and manage prototypes and samples until you approve.

  3. 3

    Produce and inspect

    We follow production on the ground and check quality against your specification before goods leave the factory.

  4. 4

    Store and deliver

    Goods move into our warehouse or straight to you, by air or sea, with the paperwork handled.
